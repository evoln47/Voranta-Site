AIPQ

AI Production Quotient

Working framework · founding cohort in formation

AIPQ is a workflow-level framework for diagnosing enterprise AI conversion. Most enterprise AI initiatives succeed in pilot but never reach production. AIPQ surfaces the six structural conditions that determine whether a workflow will convert: at the workflow level, not the org level, and without requiring pilot data.

01 Workflow Stability Do procedures change faster than AI can learn?
02 Data Readiness Is signal quality sufficient to train on?
03 Exception Tolerance What's the cost of being wrong?
04 Integration Density How many systems must AI reach across?
05 Adoption Surface Who has to change behavior, and how many?
06 Outcome Measurability Can production value be cleanly attributed?
