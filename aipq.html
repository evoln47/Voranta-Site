How AIPQ is built

The bar AIPQ is held to.

Four standards. AIPQ meets all of them. Or it doesn't ship.

Opinionated

Every framework takes a position.

Voranta frameworks aren't checklists. They name a structural problem, take a position on what causes it, and give the buyer a diagnosis sharp enough to act on. If a framework doesn't tell the buyer something true they didn't already know, it isn't worth publishing.

Transparent

Every methodology is public.

Dimensions, scoring weights, and reasoning live in the open, so buyers can see exactly how their answers map to their score and sponsors can see exactly what they're licensing. If a framework's logic can't survive being seen, it shouldn't be deployed.

Independent

The framework speaks for itself. So your sales team doesn't have to.

Sponsors get exclusivity, the brand association, and the credibility of an independently held methodology. Buyers can stress-test the scoring and ask the hard questions. The answers hold up because they weren't written to flatter anyone. Your team gets to point to the framework instead of defending it.

Living

Every framework is versioned and updated.

Frameworks evolve as evidence accumulates and the category changes. Versions are tracked and updates are published with the reasoning behind each one. A framework that hasn't moved in twelve months has stopped doing its job.

Own the framework. Own the category.

One sponsor per vertical. Once your category is licensed, it's yours for the term, including the framework, the benchmark, and the published research.
